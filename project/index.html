PROJECT 01 · MEASUREMENT & SIGNAL

Low-cost measurement chains for industrial bearings.

Industrial machinery fails quietly — bearings spall, race surfaces pit, vibration signatures shift days before catastrophic failure. The thesis: can a sub-$200 sensor stack and DSP front-end resolve those signatures well enough to predict failure on real production lines?

I'm Reuben — aerospace engineering at UNSW Sydney, graduating end of FY26. The thesis is bearing condition monitoring; before that I led the groundstation team at BlueSAT through a v0→v1 rebuild (2024 — 25), and worked as an undergrad engineer on the Nelligen Bridge replacement with Seymour Whyte.

I like systems where the physics, the electronics, and the software all have to agree before anything actually works. The hook went in early — F1 in Schools, 2017 — 18, SEA finals winner at GIS Malaysia. Haven't put it down since.
