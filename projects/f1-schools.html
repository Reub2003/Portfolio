F1 in Schools.

SEA Finals Winner · GIS Malaysia

DomainCAD · CFD · Manufacturing
RoleTeam member
Period2017 — 2018
StatusCOMPLETE

Team Velocitas. School round win 2017, SEA Regional Finals win at GIS Malaysia 2018. First real exposure to CFD, CNC, and a competition deadline that didn't move.

F1 in Schools is an international STEM competition where student teams design, manufacture, and race miniature CO₂-powered Formula 1 cars. Teams are judged on race performance, engineering design, portfolio, and verbal presentation — as close to a real engineering project as you can get at school level.

The 2017 car was my first serious attempt at engineering design — first exposure to CFD simulation and CNC manufacturing, and where it all clicked. Won the school round. 2018 was a redesign from the ground up: refined aerodynamic shaping, tighter manufacturing tolerances, and a result. Won school again, then took out the SEA Regional Finals at GIS Kuala Lumpur.

The team went on to the world finals in Abu Dhabi in 2019 — met Toto Wolff and Lewis Hamilton at the Mercedes AMG F1 camp. I didn't follow them across. I was U18 rugby captain at GIS that year and chose the season. Different sport, same lesson: small team, hard deadline, hardware that either works or it doesn't.

/ 01 — APPROACH

Simulate, cut,
race.

Iterated between CFD simulation and physical design — running flow simulations to understand drag sources, then refining the geometry and feeding changes back into the model. CNC-machined balsa car bodies, combined with CO₂ propulsion. The 2018 car was a ground-up redesign taking every lesson from 2017: better aero shaping, tighter tolerances, less drag.

/ 02 — RESULT

School win, then
SEA finals.

2017: won the school round. 2018: won school again, then took out the SEA Regional Finals at GIS Kuala Lumpur. The team carried the design to the world finals in Abu Dhabi in 2019, where they met Toto Wolff and Lewis Hamilton at the Mercedes AMG F1 camp.
